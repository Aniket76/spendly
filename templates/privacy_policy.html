Privacy Policy

How Spendly handles your information

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly ("we", "us") collects, why we collect it and how it is used when you use the Spendly personal finance tracker (the "Service"). By using the Service you acknowledge the practices described here.

Spendly is still under active development. This policy describes how the Service is intended to work and will be updated as features are added.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the Service. This falls into two groups: the personal information you give us when you create an account, and the financial records you choose to enter while using the app.

3. Personal Information

When you register, we ask for:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • A password

We use this information to create your account and let you sign in.

4. Financial Information and Transaction Records

Spendly lets you record expenses, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. This information is entered manually by you. Spendly does not connect to your bank accounts, cards or other financial institutions, and does not request your bank login details or card numbers.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store your expense records and show them back to you, including summaries of your spending.
  • Operate, maintain and improve the Service.
  • Respond to questions or requests you send us.

6. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your account details and expense records are stored in the Service's application database. We aim to limit access to this data to what is needed to run the Service.

No method of storing or transmitting data over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please choose a strong, unique password and keep it private.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal or financial information. We do not share it with third parties for advertising. We may disclose information only if required to do so by law or to protect the rights, safety or security of the Service and its users.

8. Third-Party Services

Spendly's pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, it connects to Google's servers, and Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Spendly does not currently use other third-party analytics, advertising or payment services. If that changes, this policy will be updated.

9. Cookies and Session Data

Cookies or similar session data may be used to keep you signed in and to make the Service work correctly. We do not use cookies for advertising. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies, but parts of the Service may not work properly if you do.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account and expense records for as long as your account is active, so that you can continue to use the Service. If you would like your account and associated data deleted, contact us using the details below and we will handle your request.

11. Your Privacy Rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Delete your account and associated data.

The rights available to you may depend on where you live. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

12. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update this page. Please review it periodically. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
